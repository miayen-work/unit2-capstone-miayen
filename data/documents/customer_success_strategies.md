# Customer Success Strategies

Cloudly's customer success team focuses on three main strategies to reduce churn and grow
existing accounts: proactive health checks, structured onboarding, and renewal outreach.

**Health checks**: every Pro and Enterprise account is reviewed monthly using a simple health
score based on login frequency and support ticket volume. Accounts that go quiet for more than
three weeks are flagged for a check-in call before they become a churn risk.

**Quarterly business reviews (QBRs)**: Enterprise accounts get a QBR each quarter to review
usage, discuss upcoming needs, and identify upsell opportunities (e.g. moving a Basic account
to Pro). Basic and Pro accounts do not currently get a QBR, which the team has flagged as a
gap for accounts nearing the Enterprise threshold.

**Renewal outreach**: customer success reaches out 30 days before a subscription's renewal
date to review satisfaction and address any open issues before the renewal decision is made.
Accounts with an open complaint at renewal time are a known driver of cancellations.
