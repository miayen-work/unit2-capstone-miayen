# Vacation and Time Off Policy

Full-time Cloudly employees accrue 15 paid vacation days per year, in addition to
10 company holidays and unlimited sick leave.

Vacation requests should be submitted in the HR system at least two weeks in advance
for approval by a direct manager. Requests of five or more consecutive days require
an additional two weeks of notice to allow for coverage planning.

Unused vacation days roll over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following calendar
year; any balance above that is forfeited on December 31st.
