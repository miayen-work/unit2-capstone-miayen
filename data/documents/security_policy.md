# Information Security Policy

All Cloudly employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) on their company
email, Slack, and GitHub accounts. Company laptops are encrypted by default and must
never be left unattended in public spaces.

Customer data may only be accessed through approved internal tools, and any export
of customer data to a local machine requires written approval from the security team.
Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@cloudly.example within one
hour of discovery.

Password managers are provided to all employees, and password reuse across personal
and work accounts is strictly prohibited.
