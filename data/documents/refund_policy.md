# Refund Policy

Cloudly offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on all subscription plans. Customers who
cancel within 30 days of their initial signup are eligible for a full refund of their
most recent payment.

Refund requests must be submitted through the billing portal or by emailing
billing@cloudly.example. Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days and
returned to the original payment method.

Refunds are not available for add-on services (custom onboarding, dedicated support)
or for any period beyond the most recent billing cycle. Annual plans are refundable
on a prorated basis only within the first 30 days of the annual term.
