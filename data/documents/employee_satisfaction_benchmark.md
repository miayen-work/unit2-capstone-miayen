# Employee Satisfaction Benchmarks

According to a 2025 industry survey of SaaS companies of similar size to Cloudly, the average
employee satisfaction score is 7.4 out of 10, with top-quartile companies scoring above 8.5.

Common drivers of higher satisfaction in the survey included flexible vacation policies,
structured onboarding for new hires, and clear paths for internal growth. Companies with
rigid time-off approval processes or onboarding longer than three weeks tended to score
below the industry average.
