# Expense Approval Policy

Employees may submit expense requests through the finance portal for business-related
purchases (travel, software, client meals, equipment).

Expense requests of $500 or more require sign-off from the employee's manager before
reimbursement. Requests of $5,000 or more additionally require approval from a department
director. Requests under $500 are auto-approved and reimbursed on the next payroll cycle.

All expense requests must include a receipt and a brief business justification. Requests
without a receipt are capped at $50 regardless of amount.
