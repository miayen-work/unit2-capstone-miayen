# New Employee Onboarding

New hires at Cloudly go through a two-week onboarding process before joining their
team's regular sprint cycle.

Week one covers company orientation, account and tool provisioning (email, Slack,
GitHub, and the internal wiki), and mandatory security training. Week two pairs the
new hire with a mentor on their team for shadowing and a small starter project.

IT provisions laptops and accounts on the Monday before the new hire's start date.
Managers are responsible for scheduling the first 1:1 within the new hire's first
three days.
