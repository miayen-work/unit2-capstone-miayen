# Customer Complaint Handling

Complaints can come in through the support inbox, the in-app help widget, or social media.
Whichever channel receives it, the complaint should be logged in the support system within
the same business day.

Support agents acknowledge the complaint within 24 hours and aim to resolve straightforward
issues (billing questions, minor bugs) within 3 business days. Complaints involving a
potential refund, data issue, or repeated failure for the same customer are escalated to a
team lead, who owns the resolution and follow-up with the customer.

All complaints are reviewed weekly by the support team to identify recurring issues that
should be raised with product or engineering.
